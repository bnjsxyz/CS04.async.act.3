Stage 2 • Build the foundation

Life Skills: Knowledge Put Into Action

Life skills are abilities, attitudes, and socio-emotional competencies that help people handle everyday demands, make informed decisions, build positive relationships, and respond productively to change.

🧠

Think

Understand information, examine evidence, create ideas, and solve problems.

❤️

Feel and relate

Recognize emotions, show empathy, communicate, and build healthy relationships.

🛠️

Act

Make responsible choices and turn knowledge into practical action.

Important: Knowing the definition of resilience is not the same as showing resilience. A skill becomes meaningful when it guides what you actually do.

Personal and Social Domain

The personal domain concerns how you understand and manage yourself. The social domain concerns how you interact with others and participate in your community.

Self-awarenessCommunicationResilienceNegotiationEmpathyParticipation

1. Notice

Self-awareness: Recognize what you are thinking, feeling, and doing.

2. Accept

Self-acceptance: Acknowledge yourself honestly without harsh self-judgment.

3. Choose

Self-regulation: Pause and select a response that supports your goals and respects others.
